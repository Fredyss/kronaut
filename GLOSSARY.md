# Glossary

**Post:** one piece of content a workspace member or agent writes once and publishes to one or more channels. It moves from draft through approval to scheduled and published.

**Destination:** one post on one channel. A post has a destination for each channel it goes to; each destination is scheduled, published, failed or canceled on its own.

**Campaign:** in agent tools, one post with its destinations across channels. Not a series of posts.

**Channel:** one connected account, page or board on a social network that posts are published to.

**Brand:** a group of channels for one client or product, which can have its own approval policy.

**Saved text:** a signature or template a member writes once and reuses, optionally added automatically to posts on chosen channels.

**Approval:** a person's decision that a post may be scheduled as it is. Only people approve; an API key or agent never does.

**API key:** a credential a person creates for a program, holding a subset of that person's rights in one workspace.
