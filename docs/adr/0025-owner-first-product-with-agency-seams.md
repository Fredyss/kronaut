# ADR-0025: Owner-first product with agency seams

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Deciders:** Frederik Schlentc
- **Area:** Providers & product
- **Spec:** [Overview](../architecture/README.md), [Roadmap](../architecture/09-roadmap.md)

## Context

FSL already blocks competing SaaS; a crippled Lite edition costs trust.

## Decision

**Owner-first (self-host → hosted), built with agency seams.** Self-host gets **everything free**: all providers, workspaces, approvals, MCP; no license keys, no phone-home. Paid = hosted Kronaut, whose moat is **platform-reviewed apps**

## Options considered

- **Chosen:** the decision above.
- Rejected: agency-first hosted v1
- Rejected: feature-gated self-host

## Consequences

- Revisit when: 8–12 interviews at the Phase 2→3 gate
